Multidisciplinary Visual Artist

Work that moves between mediums, held together by a single line of thought.

Mojdeh Mirzaei works across painting, drawing and sculpture, alongside a separate practice in illustration. Each body of work is built around a shared name, concept, or material, rather than a single technique.
