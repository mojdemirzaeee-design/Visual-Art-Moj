About

Mojdeh Mirzaei

Mojdeh Mirzaei is a multidisciplinary visual artist working across painting and sculpture. Her practice draws on academic training in illustration, hands-on experience in contemporary art galleries and large-scale mirror mosaic installation, and years of independent travel, using layered surfaces and sculptural form to examine loss, relational experience, and self-recognition.

Biography

She holds a degree in Graphic Design with a specialization in Illustration from the University of Applied Science and Technology (2013–2015). Her academic training introduced her to narrative construction, formal composition, and structural thinking — elements that continue to inform the framework of her visual language.

In 2016, a commissioned illustration project for a psychology book, although never published, initiated an independent research trajectory centered on loss, relational dynamics, and the experiential certainties of human life. Through sustained reading, observation, and studio experimentation, her practice gradually shifted from representational illustration toward a more psychologically driven and materially engaged approach. Painting and sculpture became primary tools for self-examination and emotional articulation rather than image production alone.

Between 2017 and 2019, Mirzaei worked within a contemporary art gallery in Isfahan, gaining practical insight into exhibition-making, curatorial processes, and the professional ecology of contemporary art. This experience strengthened her institutional awareness while clarifying her commitment to developing an independent studio-based practice.

Since 2019, periods of solitary travel within Iran and abroad have functioned as lived research. Direct encounters with diverse environments and social narratives have expanded her perception of relational experience, cultural coexistence, and shared vulnerability. These observations inform her work not as documentation, but as internalized material rearticulated through painting and sculptural form.

Alongside her studio practice, Mirzaei trained in the historic Iranian technique of mirror mosaic (Ayeneh-kari). She subsequently executed large-scale commissioned installations in architectural interiors in Isfahan. Working at scale within active, high-traffic environments strengthened her technical precision, material management, spatial coordination, and on-site execution.

Her recent bodies of work integrate academic inquiry, field observation, and sustained studio research. Through layered surfaces, expressive gestures, and sculptural construction, she examines processes of self-recognition and emotional exposure beyond inherited social and cultural frameworks. For Mirzaei, clarity of self-perception becomes a prerequisite for perceiving others with attentiveness and responsibility.
