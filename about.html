Moj

Moj is a multidisciplinary artist working across painting, textile, photography, and digital media. Rather than organizing work by medium, each body of work is built around a single name, concept, or question, and follows it into whatever material fits it best — which is why a single idea might surface as a canvas, a woven sculpture, and a video loop within the same year.
