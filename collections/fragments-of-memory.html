Fragments of Memory

Fragments of Memory began as an attempt to paint something that resists a single, stable image: the way a memory arrives in pieces, reordered and half-invented each time it's recalled. Each canvas layers paint over torn paper, old fabric, and photographic transfers, then deliberately obscures parts of what came before.

The work isn't interested in restoring a clear picture of the past. It's interested in the residue — the parts that survive erasure, and the gaps that get filled in with something new. Across the collection, recurring marks and colors return in altered form, the way a memory returns changed by the person remembering it.
