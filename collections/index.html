Collections

Five bodies of work, each built around one idea across mediums.

In-Between-ness

Painting & Drawing — 2025–Present

The humor of death and the seriousness of life, held inside the opened body of a fish.

Clowns Series

Drawings & Cut Playing Cards — 2019–Present

Imagined clown figures and cut cards, between absurdity and sincerity.

Hands Series

Rapid Drawings & Ceramic Sculptures — 2017–2023

Bodily memory and the traces left by lived experience — the hand as a site where repetition, suffering, joy, and identity are inscribed.

Flowers Series

Colored Pencil on Paper — 2022

Hybrid portraits merging human and floral forms, each flower a portrait of someone remembered.

The Little Fire and The Little Girl

Printmaking & Mixed Media — 2015

A children's picture book following a small flame searching for its place in the world.
