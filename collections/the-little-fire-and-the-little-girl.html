The Little Fire and The Little Girl

Children’s picture book · Author and Illustrator · Forthcoming from a European publisher

This illustrated story follows a small flame searching for its place in the world. Rather than presenting identity as something fixed, the narrative approaches it as a process of encounter and transformation. Through printmaking, collage, and mixed media, familiar materials become active storytellers, revealing how relationships and imagination can reshape our understanding of self, others, and belonging.
