Static Noise

Static Noise takes interference — the glitch, the dropped frame, the signal lost between broadcast and receiver — as a subject in its own right rather than an error to correct. Working with generative video, screen captures, and manipulated broadcast footage, the collection treats corruption as a kind of drawing.

Each video loop is built from source material that has been compressed, degraded, and recompressed until the artifacts of that process become the image itself. The result sits somewhere between documentation and abstraction, asking what's left of a picture once the channel carrying it starts to fail.
