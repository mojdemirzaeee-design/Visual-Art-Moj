Liminal Spaces

Liminal Spaces is a study of the places we pass through but never stay in — stairwells, hallways, waiting rooms, the strip of pavement between one building and the next. Photographed in long exposures and re-staged as physical installation, these images ask what happens to a place once its only function is transition.

The photographs are printed at architectural scale and installed to interrupt the very kind of space they depict, so that the viewer's body becomes part of the threshold being examined. Nothing in the collection resolves into a destination; every image is a doorway to another image.
