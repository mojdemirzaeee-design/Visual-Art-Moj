Woven Silence

Woven Silence treats thread as a language for what refuses to be spoken aloud. Each piece begins on a loom and ends as a free-standing sculptural form — knotted, tensioned, sometimes torn back out and rewoven — so that the process of making mirrors the work's real subject: things withheld, repaired, and withheld again.

The palette across the collection is intentionally restrained, letting structure and texture carry the weight that color usually would. Where a thread breaks, it stays broken; the mend is left visible rather than hidden, treating repair as part of the image rather than its erasure.
